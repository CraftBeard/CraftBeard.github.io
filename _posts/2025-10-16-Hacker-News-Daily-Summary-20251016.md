### 19. Looking at kmalloc() and the SLUB Memory Allocator (2019)

 > The article explains how `kmalloc(128, GFP_KERNEL)` works in the Linux kernel using the SLUB allocator. SLUB pre-allocates slabs of fixed-size objects to efficiently handle small memory requests. The allocation process involves finding the appropriate slab cache for 128-byte objects, retrieving a free object from the freelist, and updating metadata atomically without locks.
