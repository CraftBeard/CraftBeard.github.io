### 7. Reservoir Sampling

 Score: 395 Comments: 73 [Link](https://samwho.dev/reservoir-sampling/)

 > Reservoir sampling is a technique for selecting a fair random sample from an unknown-sized set. It works by giving each new item a `1/n` chance to replace the current selection, ensuring equal probability for all items. This method is useful for scenarios like log collection, where you need to sample data without knowing the total volume in advance. The algorithm can be extended to select multiple items by adjusting probabilities accordingly. It’s efficient and predictable in memory usage.
