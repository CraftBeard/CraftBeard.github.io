### 3. What Is the Fourier Transform?

 Score: 284 Comments: 117 [Link](https://www.quantamagazine.org/what-is-the-fourier-transform-20250903/)

 > The Fourier transform is a mathematical technique that decomposes any function into its constituent frequencies, much like how the ear separates sound into different pitches. Developed by Joseph Fourier in the 19th century, it has become a foundational tool in fields ranging from physics to data compression. The transform works by scanning all possible frequencies to determine how much each one contributes to the original function. Its applications are vast, including image compression (JPEGs), audio processing, and quantum mechanics.
