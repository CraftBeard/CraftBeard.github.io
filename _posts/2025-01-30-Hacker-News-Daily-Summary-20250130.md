### 1. Investigating the case of human nose shape and climate adaptation

 Score: 27 Comments: discuss [Link](https://journals.plos.org/plosgenetics/article?id=10.1371/journal.pgen.1006616)

 > This study investigates whether human nose shape variation across populations is influenced by climate adaptation. Using Qst–Fst comparisons, the researchers found that nares width and alar base width show greater differentiation than expected under genetic drift alone, suggesting local adaptation. They also found that nares width correlates with temperature and absolute humidity, supporting the hypothesis that climate has shaped nose morphology. However, the study acknowledges that other factors, such as sexual selection, may also play a role in nose shape evolution.
