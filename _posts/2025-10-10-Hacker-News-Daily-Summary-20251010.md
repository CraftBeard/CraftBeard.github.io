### 26. The Burrows-Wheeler Transform

 Score: 128 Comments: 27 [Link](https://sandbox.bio/concepts/bwt)

 > The Burrows-Wheeler Transform (BWT) is an algorithm that rearranges strings to group identical characters together, making it useful for data compression and sequence alignment. It works by creating all rotations of a string, sorting them, and taking the last column as the transformed output. The transform is reversible, allowing recovery of the original string. BWT enables efficient substring searches through its last-to-first mapping property. It powers tools like bzip2 for compression and bowtie/bwa for genomic sequence alignment.
