 > This article argues that forcing LLMs to write terse code, like the compact style traditionally preferred in q/kdb+, reduces their accuracy. Using information theory, it explains that compressing the same information into fewer tokens increases perplexity (average surprisal per token), making the code harder for LLMs to generate and process reliably.
