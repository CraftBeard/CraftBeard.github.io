### 10. The history and use of /etc./glob in early Unixes

 Score: 27 Comments: 13 [Link](https://utcc.utoronto.ca/~cks/space/blog/unix/EtcGlobHistory)

 > The article discusses the history of `/etc/glob`, an external program used in early Unix versions (V1-V6) to handle shell wildcard expansion, before the V7 Bourne shell integrated this functionality. `/etc/glob` processed command arguments for wildcards and executed the command with expanded arguments. Early versions had quirks with quoted wildcards, later resolved using an 8th-bit hack to mark quoted characters. The separation of globbing into `/etc/glob` may have been to keep the shell minimal. The feature dates back to Unix V1 and was eventually integrated into the shell in V7.
