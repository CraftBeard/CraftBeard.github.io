### 12. What Is the Fourier Transform?

 Score: 26 Comments: 2 [Link](https://www.quantamagazine.org/what-is-the-fourier-transform-20250903/)

 > The Fourier transform is a mathematical technique that decomposes any function into its constituent frequencies, much like the ear separates sound into different pitches. Developed by Joseph Fourier in the 19th century, it has become a foundational tool in fields ranging from mathematics and physics to data compression and signal processing. It works by scanning all possible frequencies to determine how much each one contributes to the original function. This revolutionary concept allows complex signals to be broken down and analyzed in the frequency domain.
