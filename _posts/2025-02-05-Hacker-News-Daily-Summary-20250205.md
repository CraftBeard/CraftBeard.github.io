### 16. MTR: 'traceroute' and 'ping' in a single tool

 > MTR is a network diagnostic tool that combines the functionalities of 'traceroute' and 'ping' to analyze the connection between a host and a specified destination. It can be compiled easily and is available in several binary distributions. The tool was originally written by Matt Kimball and is now maintained by Roger Wolff.
