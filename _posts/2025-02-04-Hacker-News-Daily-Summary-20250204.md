### 1. Alibaba/T-HEAD's Xuantie C910

 Score: 151 Comments: 21 [Link](https://chipsandcheese.com/p/alibabat-heads-xuantie-c910)

 > The Xuantie C910, developed by Alibaba's subsidiary T-HEAD, is a high-performance RISC-V core designed for applications like AI, edge computing, and industrial control. It features a 12-stage out-of-order pipeline, supports RISC-V's vector extension, and targets low power and area efficiency. However, its performance is hindered by a poorly balanced out-of-order engine and weak memory subsystem, particularly its slow and small L2 cache. Despite these limitations, the C910 represents a significant step in China's push for domestic chip development and reducing reliance on foreign technology.
