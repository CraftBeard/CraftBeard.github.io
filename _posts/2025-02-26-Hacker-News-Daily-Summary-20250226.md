### 20. Why are QR Codes with capital letters smaller than QR codes with lower case?

 Score: 216 Comments: 47 [Link](https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2025/02/why-are-qr-codes-with-capital-letters-smaller-than-qr-codes-with-lower-case-letters/)

 > QR codes with uppercase letters are smaller because they use Alphanumeric mode, which efficiently encodes a limited character set (including uppercase letters and symbols) using fewer bits. Lowercase letters require Byte mode, which uses more bits per character, leading to larger QR codes. The Alphanumeric mode's optimized encoding allows uppercase URLs to fit into a smaller QR version (Type 1) compared to lowercase (Type 2). This efficiency stems from the QR specification's design prioritizing common characters for compactness. Thus, using uppercase letters reduces QR code size when URLs fit the Alphanumeric character set.
