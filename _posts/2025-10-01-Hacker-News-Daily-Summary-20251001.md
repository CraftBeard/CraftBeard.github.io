### 2. Category Theory Illustrated – Natural Transformations

 Score: 11 Comments: discuss [Link](https://abuseofnotation.github.io/category-theory-illustrated/11_natural_transformations/)

 > Natural transformations are morphisms between functors that connect their outputs in a consistent way, ensuring all relevant diagrams commute. They form the foundation for defining categorical equivalence, where two categories are considered equivalent if they can be connected by functors whose compositions are naturally isomorphic to identity functors. In programming, natural transformations correspond to polymorphic functions that operate uniformly across types. This concept highlights category theory's focus on structure and relationships rather than individual objects.
