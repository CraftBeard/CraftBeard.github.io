### 14. Simulating Time in Square-Root Space

 Score: 69 Comments: 12 [Link](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2025/017/)

 > This technical report presents a significant improvement in simulating multitape Turing machines, showing that any machine running in time \( t(n) \geq n \) can be simulated in \( O(\sqrt{t \log t}) \) space. This surpasses the long-standing \( O(t/\log t) \) space bound from Hopcroft, Paul, and Valiant (1977). The result enables evaluating size-\( s \) circuits in \( \sqrt{s} \cdot \text{poly}(\log s) \) space and provides explicit problems requiring near-quadratic time on multitape machines in linear space, advancing understanding of **P vs PSPACE**. The proof leverages space-efficient algorithms for Tree Evaluation problems (Cook and Mertz, 2024). The work marks progress in time-space tradeoffs and computational complexity lower bounds.
