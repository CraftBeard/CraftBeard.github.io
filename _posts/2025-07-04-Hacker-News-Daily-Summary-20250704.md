### 15. Context Engineering for Agents

 Score: 41 Comments: 8 [Link](https://rlancemartin.github.io/2025/06/23/context_engineering/)

 > Context engineering is the practice of managing an LLM's limited context window to optimize agent performance. It involves four key strategies: writing (saving context externally), selecting (pulling relevant context), compressing (summarizing or trimming), and isolating (splitting context across agents or environments). These techniques help agents handle long-running tasks efficiently while avoiding issues like context overload or distraction. Effective context engineering is crucial for building high-performing AI agents.
