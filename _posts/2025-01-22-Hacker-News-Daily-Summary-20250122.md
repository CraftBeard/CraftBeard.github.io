### 29. Concept cells help your brain abstract information and build memories

 > Concept cells are specialized neurons in the brain that fire in response to specific concepts, such as people, objects, or places, regardless of how the concept is presented (e.g., through images, words, or sounds). Discovered in the early 2000s, these cells play a critical role in abstracting information and forming memories by linking related concepts.
