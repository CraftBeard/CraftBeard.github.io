### 30. Why do some birds mimic the sounds of other species?

 Score: 3 Comments: 1 comment [Link](https://www.allaboutbirds.org/news/why-do-some-birds-mimic-the-sounds-of-other-species/)

 > Some birds mimic the sounds of other species for various reasons, such as attracting mates, defending territories, or avoiding detection as intruders. For example, Northern Mockingbirds mimic sounds to impress females and secure better territories, while Indigobirds mimic host species to avoid being recognized as parasites. Mimicry can also serve as a defense mechanism, like the Thick-billed Euphonia imitating alarm calls to deter predators. Occasionally, mimicry results from errors in song learning, but such mistakes are rarely passed on due to strong selection pressures.
