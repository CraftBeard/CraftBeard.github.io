### 5. QUIC for the kernel

 Score: 266 Comments: 194 [Link](https://lwn.net/Articles/1029851/)

 > Xin Long has proposed the first set of patches to add QUIC protocol support to the Linux kernel, addressing TCP's latency, head-of-line blocking, and ossification issues. QUIC, built on UDP with built-in encryption, is widely used in user space (e.g., web browsers) but lacks kernel integration. The patch introduces `IPPROTO_QUIC` for socket operations, offloading TLS setup to user space. Initial benchmarks show performance gaps compared to TCP, but hardware offloads and optimizations may improve this. Adoption is growing, with Samba and curl exploring support, though full integration may take until 2026.
