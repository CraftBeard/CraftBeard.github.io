### 28. The McPhee method for writing deeply reported nonfiction

 Score: 162 Comments: 41 [Link](https://jsomers.net/blog/the-mcphee-method)

 > The McPhee method is a four-stage process for writing deeply reported nonfiction. It involves first gathering a massive amount of notes through immersive reporting. Next, the writer selects the best notes and organizes them into thematic buckets. In the third stage, these buckets are structured into a logical outline. Finally, the writer drafts the piece, using the pre-arranged notes to avoid a blank page. This method distributes the hardest work across the process, making the actual writing phase more manageable.
