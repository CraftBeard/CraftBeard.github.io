 > Nuclear reactor lasers convert fission energy directly into laser light, bypassing electrical conversion steps, offering potential mass and efficiency benefits for space applications. Methods include direct pumping via fission fragments or neutrons and indirect methods using reactor heat to generate light for optical pumping.
