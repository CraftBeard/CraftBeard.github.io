### 7. Value-Based Deep RL Scales Predictably

 Score: 41 Comments: 2 [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04327)

 > The paper "Value-Based Deep RL Scales Predictably" by Oleh Rybkin et al. demonstrates that value-based off-policy reinforcement learning (RL) methods exhibit predictable scaling behavior, contrary to common beliefs about their instability. The authors identify a Pareto frontier for data and compute requirements, enabling performance prediction from small-scale experiments. They propose optimal resource allocation strategies and validate their findings using SAC, BRO, and PQL algorithms across various environments. This predictability is achieved by managing overfitting and plasticity loss through hyperparameter relationships.

### 9. A brief history of code signing at Mozilla

 Score: 208 Comments: 69 [Link](https://hearsum.ca/posts/history-of-code-signing-at-mozilla/)

 > Mozilla's code signing process for Firefox has evolved significantly over the past 20 years, starting with manual GPG and Authenticode signing on Windows machines and progressing to automated, secure, and scalable systems like Autograph. Key milestones include parallelized signing, Linux-based Windows signing, macOS .app signing, and the adoption of Taskcluster for CI/CD. Modern tools like Autograph and rcodesign have enhanced security and efficiency, enabling Mozilla to sign thousands of artifacts daily with minimal human intervention.
