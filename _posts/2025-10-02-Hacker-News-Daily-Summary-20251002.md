### 2. F3: Open-source data file format for the future [pdf]

 Score: 162 Comments: 70 [Link](https://db.cs.cmu.edu/papers/2025/zeng-sigmod2025.pdf)

 > F3 is a next-generation open-source columnar file format designed for interoperability, extensibility, and efficiency. It addresses limitations in older formats like Parquet and ORC by decoupling I/O units from logical row groups and enabling flexible dictionary scopes. F3 embeds WebAssembly (Wasm) decoding binaries within each file, ensuring compatibility across platforms and allowing easy integration of new encoding schemes. Evaluations show F3 matches state-of-the-art formats in performance while providing superior future-proofing.

### 28. Category Theory Illustrated – Natural Transformations

 Score: 196 Comments: 75 [Link](https://abuseofnotation.github.io/category-theory-illustrated/11_natural_transformations/)

 > Natural transformations are morphisms between functors that connect corresponding objects in a systematic way, ensuring all relevant diagrams commute. They form the foundation for defining categorical equivalence, where two categories are considered equivalent if there exist functors between them whose compositions are naturally isomorphic to identity functors. In programming, natural transformations correspond to polymorphic functions that operate uniformly across different types. The concept reveals that category theory focuses more on relationships between objects than on the objects themselves.
