### 1. Mathematicians Discover New Way for Spheres to 'Kiss'

 Score: 48 Comments: 19 [Link](https://www.quantamagazine.org/mathematicians-discover-new-way-for-spheres-to-kiss-20250115/)

 > Mathematicians Anqi Li and Henry Cohn have made significant progress on the "kissing number" problem, which asks how many identical spheres can touch a central sphere without overlapping. By breaking away from traditional symmetric arrangements and introducing irregular structures, they improved lower bounds for the kissing number in dimensions 17 through 21, marking the first progress in these dimensions since the 1960s. Their work highlights the potential of less symmetric, more disordered configurations in solving long-standing mathematical challenges.
