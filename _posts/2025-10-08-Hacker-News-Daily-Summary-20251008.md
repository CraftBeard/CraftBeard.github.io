### 26. How does gradient descent work?

 Score: 254 Comments: 20 [Link](https://centralflows.github.io/part1/)

 > Gradient descent in deep learning doesn't stay in the "stable region" as traditional theory suggests. Instead, it operates at the "edge of stability" where sharpness (largest Hessian eigenvalue) hovers around 2/η. When sharpness exceeds this threshold, oscillations along the top eigenvector automatically reduce sharpness through third-order effects. This creates a self-correcting mechanism where gradient descent dynamically regulates sharpness while continuing to optimize.
