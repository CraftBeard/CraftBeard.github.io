### 11. Normal-order syntax-rules and proving the fix-point of call/cc

 Score: 14 Comments: discuss [Link](https://okmij.org/ftp/Scheme/callcc-calc-page.html)

 > This article presents a formal proof that repeated applications of `call/cc` (call-with-current-continuation) in Scheme yield a fix-point equivalent to self-application. It uses a CPS (continuation-passing style) transformation and a direct-style beta-normalizer implemented via Scheme's syntax-rules macros to demonstrate the equivalence. The proof shows that expressions like `((call/cc call/cc) p)` and `((lambda (x) (x x)) p)` are observationally equivalent when `p` is a value. The work highlights the use of macros as a proof assistant for lambda-calculus reasoning.
