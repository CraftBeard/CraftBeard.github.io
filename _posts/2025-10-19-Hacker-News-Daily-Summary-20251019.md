### 9. When you opened a screen shot of a video in Paint, the video was playing in it

 Score: 257 Comments: 39 [Link](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/oldnewthing/20251014-00/?p=111681)

 > This article explains that older video players used a technique called "overlays" where they drew solid green pixels on screen and instructed the graphics card to replace them with actual video frames. When you took a screenshot, it captured these green pixels instead of the video. If you opened this screenshot in Paint while the video player was still active, the graphics card would again replace the green pixels with the live video frames, making it appear as if the static image was playing. This was a performance optimization that's no longer used in modern systems with desktop compositors.
