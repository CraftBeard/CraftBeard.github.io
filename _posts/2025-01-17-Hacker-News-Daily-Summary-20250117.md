### 8. Bypassing disk encryption on systems with automatic TPM2 unlock

 Score: 154 Comments: 73 [Link](https://oddlama.org/blog/bypassing-disk-encryption-with-tpm2-unlock/)

 > Many systems using TPM2 for automatic disk decryption are vulnerable to a filesystem confusion attack, allowing attackers with brief physical access to decrypt the disk. This occurs because the initrd fails to verify the LUKS identity of the decrypted partition, enabling attackers to create a fake partition and extract the original LUKS key from the TPM.
