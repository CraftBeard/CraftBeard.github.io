### 21. How to make a Smith chart

 Score: 123 Comments: 22 [Link](https://www.johndcook.com/blog/2025/10/23/smith-chart/)

 > The Smith chart is the image of a grid in the right half-plane under the Möbius transformation f(z) = (z - 1)/(z + 1). This function maps the imaginary axis to the unit circle and the right half-plane to the interior of this circle. Vertical lines in the z-plane transform into circles inside the unit circle, all tangent at the point w = 1. Horizontal lines transform into circular arcs that intersect these vertical-image circles at right angles.
